# Privacy policy

Rotline is a game with no account, no analytics and no advertising.

## What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, scrap, upgrades, your survivor, your Endless record) and your settings are kept in a
small file in the app's own folder on your device. Removing the app with its data removes them.

## Who Rotline talks to

- **The store, only if you buy or restore something.** The email you type goes to the Hartmann Studios license
  server and to Stripe, which runs the checkout in your browser and sends your receipt. The license server keeps your
  email and what you bought so you can restore it on another device. Stripe keeps your payment details under
  [its own privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy). Rotline itself never sees your card.
- **The store's price list.** When you open the app, Rotline asks the license server for the current prices. That
  request carries no email or identifier.
- **GitHub, to check for updates.** About twice a day at most, Rotline asks GitHub for its latest release so it can
  tell you about a new version. Turn automatic checks off in Settings or the update panel.

Nothing else. Rotline has no ads and no ad identifiers, and it sends no usage data, crash reports, scores or tracking
identifiers anywhere. Playing works fully offline.
